About Us

Supporting Holistic Wellness Among Young People

Basileia Ministry Limited is a not-for-profit organization supporting the holistic wellness of marginalized young people across Uganda. We address challenges such as stress, depression, anxiety, and HIV/AIDS by creating safe spaces, offering counseling, and empowering youth with knowledge and hope. Our dedicated team of youth counselors, social workers, and mentors work across Uganda, creating safe spaces where young people feel heard, supported, and empowered.

Who We Are

Basileia is a youth-led not-for-profit organization dedicated to supporting the mental health and wellbeing of young people, especially those who are marginalized and underserved. The organization was founded after one of our co-founders, Muwaga Meddy, experienced firsthand the challenges young people face when mental health needs are misunderstood. While in high school, he struggled with severe anxiety and recurring panic attacks, yet support from schools, faith spaces, and health services was limited. After eventually receiving appropriate mental health care, this experience revealed how many young people suffer in silence. Motivated by this reality, Muwaga Muhamed and his siblings, together with other young people, founded Basileia to ensure that young people have access to understanding, counseling, and hope through compassionate and stigma-free support.

What we do

Our BHWY (Basileia Holistic Wellness program for Youths)

Mental Health Awareness and Counseling

We provide one-on-one and group counseling to help students manage emotions, build confidence, and find healthy coping strategies.

HIV/AIDS awareness

Through workshops and school campaigns, we teach accurate HIV/AIDS information, prevention, empathy, and stigma reduction.

Our Safe Circles

Our Safe Circles offer peer support spaces where youth discuss forgiveness, self-care, relationships, and life challenges openly. And our Safe Circle show aired on Rest tv.

Spiritual Growth

We encourage spiritual reflection and personal growth in faith — helping young people discover purpose and inner strength.
